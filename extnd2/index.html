TAX‑EFFICIENT WEALTH CREATION

How to keep every rupee of profit legally tax‑free

Agricultural Income Exemption (Section 10(1))

Income derived from cultivation of land is exempt from income tax. Polyhouse floriculture qualifies if it involves basic operations on land (tilling, sowing, planting, harvesting). LeanBloom units are designed as pure cultivation — no processing that would convert it to business income.

  • Own or lease rural agricultural land (no capital gains on sale of rural agri land).
  • Operate as an Individual or HUF — companies/LLPs cannot claim this exemption easily.
  • Maintain separate books, land records, cultivation proofs, and sales invoices.

Practical Compliance Checklist

  • Land document (7/12, title deed) in name of assessee.
  • Bank account for agri operations (separate from other business).
  • Daily logbook: date of planting, inputs used, harvest records, sales.
  • Photographs of polyhouse, crops at various stages.
  • GST registration not required if only fresh flowers sold (exempt). Input GST on structure/equipment may be a cost; consult CA.

Always consult a tax professional for specific structuring. This information is for educational purposes.
