PHASE 0 — THE CORE THAT WORKS

Minimum Viable Polyhouse Baseline

Target: First profitable harvest in 5-7 months

Structure & Climate

  • Naturally ventilated design — lowest capex, minimal moving parts. 6-9m span GI pipe or local frame options.
  • 200 micron UV-stabilized poly film (5-year rated). High light transmission + thermal properties.
  • Passive + manual ventilation: Side roll-ups, top vents, shade nets (50-75%). No dependency on power for basic climate control.
  • Windbreaks & site orientation optimized for local conditions to reduce film stress.
Est. structure cost: ₹750–1,100 / m²

Water & Nutrition

Drip fertigation system
Low-cost Indian/imported kits with filtration. Manual or basic timer start. Precise nutrient delivery = higher quality stems.

STUDY THE CORE RISKS — BUILD RESILIENCE IN

Malfunction Consequences & How LeanBloom Mitigates Them

Understanding what can go wrong is the foundation of a robust revenue system. The lean approach deliberately minimizes single points of failure.

HIGH IMPACT

Poly Film Tear / Degradation

Wind, hail, UV aging or poor installation causes tears. Leads to uncontrolled environment, rain ingress, disease explosion.

Revenue hit: 30–100% localized loss
MED-HIGH

Irrigation System Failure

Clogged drippers, pump failure, power cut or leak. Causes uneven moisture → root rot or drought stress. Flower quality collapses fast.

Revenue hit: 20–60% yield/quality loss
HIGH IMPACT

Temperature / Humidity Spike

Prolonged heatwave, poor ventilation or sensor failure. Botrytis, mildew, flower abortion, short stems, petal burn. Premium price lost.

Revenue hit: 25–70% quality downgrade
HIGH SPREAD

Pest Outbreak (Thrips / Mites / Whitefly)

Common in flower crops. Rapid multiplication in stable warm environment. Deformed blooms, virus transmission, export/domestic rejection.

Revenue hit: 15–80% + spray costs
OPERATIONAL

Prolonged Power Outage

Pumps stop, any fans/misters fail. Heat/humidity builds rapidly. Critical during peak summer or flowering stage.

Revenue hit: Acute stress + secondary disease
EXTERNAL

Market Price Crash / Channel Failure

Oversupply, festival timing, quality inconsistency or buyer default. Even perfect production can face realization issues.

Revenue hit: 30–50% margin compression
LeanBloom mitigation: Start simple (fewer failure points) + add monitoring early (visibility = early intervention)
